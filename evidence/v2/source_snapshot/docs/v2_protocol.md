# Extension study protocol: source coverage, representativeness and spatial adaptation

Status: development and implementation. No new-panel segmentation scores have
been inspected at creation. The execution freeze will be a separate hashed
record after development calibration and data integrity checks. This is a local
prospective record, not public preregistration.

## Why the study was reopened

The first study supplied an auditable experiment, but its eight test regions,
single source split and frozen pixel heads did not establish a sufficiently
broad contribution for a confident venue recommendation. Completing a manuscript
did not resolve those scientific limits. The user explicitly requested continued
research. Version1 manuscript, all run evidence and both delivered artifacts are
retained as historical evidence; its originally held-out regions now belong to
the extension's development set.

Direct prior work includes Nogueira et al., *Core-Set Selection for
Data-efficient Land Cover Segmentation*, IEEE Access2026,
https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3659734; the authors' method preprint is
https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01225v2. That study already compares image-based and
label-based selection with spatial segmentation networks. DSGR also compares
matched-size single/multiple source regions for classification:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-025-02518-z. Frozen-feature selection, geographic
diversity and training-subset selection are not claimed as new principles.

The extension asks whether quotas add predictive value beyond worst-case visual
coverage and mean representativeness when the source set changes and the
segmentation representation can adapt. A permuted-group control tests whether
alignment with actual geography matters. This is an empirical question; no
architectural novelty or guaranteed positive effect is assumed.

## Development and permanently separate new panel

All120 earlier images from24 regions are development/source data. Three cyclic
source allocations are generated by one seeded permutation, each with12 train,
four validation and eight unused development regions. Their exact lists and
candidate-pool seeds are in `configs/v2_design.json`. A region can have different
development roles across allocations. No old result is reclassified as a new
confirmatory test.

A fixed panel comprises24 other OpenEarthMap regions with three filename-sampled
complete RGB/mask pairs each, seed2026101802. The list is fixed before acquisition
and must not be changed based on masks, appearance, prediction or effect sizes.
These are purposively chosen geographically distributed available regions, not
a probability sample of the planet. All72 panel images remain evaluation-only
under all three source allocations. New-panel scores cannot select a policy,
budget, optimization setting, metric or retained checkpoint.

Original ZIP members are fully read into bounded RAM and checked by size/CRC;
SHA256 records are computed. Prior original files also have prebound SHA256s.
New original SHA256s are first recorded after verified retrieval, not compared
with a non-existent external per-file checksum. RGB is bilinearly resized448,
masks nearest-resized448, then stored as lossless PNG with original source and
georeference sidecars. The full archive MD5 is not recomputed. Exact decoded RGB
hashes and supported georeference envelopes will be screened across development
and panel roles. Unknown metadata remains unknown; different city names are not
proof of complete mosaic/provider/pretraining independence.

The completed pre-scoring screen found no supported/caution cross-role bounding-
box overlap candidates or exact decoded cross-role RGB duplicates. Two images
have CRS area-of-use cautions: old aachen__aachen_1 and new
monrovia__monrovia_48. The latter's257 boundary points all lie outside the stated
EPSG32636 area, so its transformed position is not reliable separation evidence.
Keep all24 regions in the fixed primary analysis; prospectively exclude the
entire Monrovia region for a23-region descriptive sensitivity, using the same
within-region averaging and paired bootstrap. This is recorded separately in
`configs/v2_geographic_sensitivity.json`, before model scores. Source0's existing
separate contrast provides a reference using no Aachen images. No image is
replaced and no coordinate system is guessed or repaired. Screening padding for
unknown operation accuracy does not guarantee positional error bounds.

## Selection and information restrictions

Source pools use the earlier36-candidate balanced3 and skewed5/1 rules, regenerated
for each source allocation using a fixed independent seed. Profile membership
can differ, so a profile contrast does not isolate imbalance as a causal factor.
All policies within a profile receive identical candidate IDs, normalized DINOv2
RGB descriptors, region IDs, a budget and a seed. No candidate masks, class counts,
source validation scores, target descriptors or target masks enter a selector.

Eight base policies are planned: random, cosine k-center, quota random, quota k-center,
permuted quota k-center, k-means++ seeding, facility location and a fixed12-cluster
feature-diversity round-robin adaptation. The latter is explicitly not the full
Vendi-selected-cluster ResNet18 FD implementation of Nogueira et al. Its controlled
DINO descriptor comparison has that adaptation boundary. Original definitions,
implementation choices and validation are in `v2_selection_methods.md`.

Before the first new-panel segmentation score, a separate prospective supplement
in `configs/v2_extension.json` adds real and permuted inverse-source-frequency
weighted facility location on skewed pools only. This keeps the base design and
ongoing development calibration identities unchanged. Covered candidate rows
receive weights1/(R*n_group); similarity, greedy maximization and tie order match
ordinary facility location. Permuted groups retain the same group sizes and
weight multiset. Balanced groups reduce exactly to ordinary facility location;
duplicate balanced fits are not executed. This is an existing weighted objective,
not a new algorithm. The same-objective real-minus-permuted comparison can assess
geographic alignment, but a comparison with quota k-center cannot isolate quota
rigidity because the underlying coverage objective also changes. Exact references
and definitions are in `v2_weighted_selection.md`.

## Trainable spatial model and development calibration

The principal extension uses an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 encoder and a
U-Net-style bilinear skip decoder (channels128/64/32/16). All encoder convolutions,
BatchNorm affine parameters and decoder parameters are optimized. Pretrained
encoder BatchNorm running statistics are fixed for batch-size2; decoder uses
GroupNorm. This is an existing design family and no new architecture is claimed.

Train with random224 crops on the448 processed grid, independent horizontal and
vertical flips with probability0.5 and uniform quarter rotations. Predict on the
whole448 grid without augmentation. Original void0 becomes ignore255 and valid
classes1..8 become0..7. Cross entropy is unweighted over valid pixels. AdamW uses
beta0.9/0.999, eps1e-8 and weight decay0.0001 on all parameters. Decoder learning
rate is ten times encoder rate; both remain constant throughout a fit. The source
validation operation changes neither crop RNG nor optimizer state.

For each source allocation separately, use one24-image random sample from its
balanced pool (selection101, initialization0). Compare encoder rates0.0001/0.0003
and checkpoints300/600/1200. Choose the maximum equal-region union-class validation
mIoU; exact ties favor fewer steps, then lower encoder rate. Only that allocation's
four old validation regions enter the decision. These shared labeled development
and calibration resources remain outside each formal fit's image count.

The base full-model matrix has three source allocations x eight policies x three
fit-seed pairs x budget12 x skewed pool =72 fits. Before any new-panel scoring,
source-only selection diagnostics found identical ordered subsets across several
FL selection repeats. To avoid repeating an identical fit, the prospective
supplement pairs selection seeds0/1/2 with optimization seeds0/1/2 for every
policy. This amends the base design's fixed initialization0 randomization without
changing that file, its calibration identities or the planned contrast/metric.
Three paired fit seeds do not separately identify selection variance and
optimization variance. A shared seed does not imply identical crops when images
and void retries differ. The source-only diagnostic and preceding supplement
version are retained in evidence/v2; no panel score informed this amendment.

The frozen DINOv2 MLP different-recipe control matrix uses the established600-step,
0.001 recipe, both pools, budgets6/12/24, all eight policies, three source
allocations and three paired fit seeds:432 fits. This inherited
development recipe is not re-tuned on the new panel. The base extension has504
fits. The prospective weighted-FL supplement adds18 full-model and54 frozen-head
fits, giving90 full-model +486 frozen-head =576 fits. The original primary
estimand and development calibration remain unchanged; the two added contrasts
are secondary descriptive analyses, not a new confirmatory family.
The old630 formal and144 pilot fits remain separate and are not pooled into the
extension estimates.

The frozen DINOv2 head and trainable ResNet18 spatial model differ in encoder
family, pretraining, grid/decoder and augmentation as well as encoder updates.
Their comparison tests policy behavior under two recorded model recipes; it does
not isolate a causal effect of fine tuning or spatial decoding.


Available CPU PyTorch can train the entire spatial model; its measured resource
cost and device are recorded honestly. The RTX4060 runs CuPy frozen heads. A100
is retried at major compute milestones, but hardware availability cannot change
the planned comparisons or cause policy-dependent device assignment.

## Primary estimand, uncertainty and interpretation

The new primary contrast is real quota k-center minus k-center for the complete
spatial model, budget12, skewed pool, equal-region union-class mIoU. Per region,
sum image confusion matrices before computing class IoUs. Union classes include
truth or predictions; classes absent from both are undefined and omitted. The
earlier truth-present convention is reported alongside, not silently replaced in
the old study. This prospective choice counts prediction-only false-positive
classes and is fixed before any new-panel predictions. Scores are at448, not the
official full-resolution OpenEarthMap leaderboard setting.

Average source allocations and paired fit seeds within each of24 panel regions,
then calculate24 matched policy differences. Use10000 percentile region-bootstrap
resamples with seed2026101805. This conditional interval is not an interval over
all possible source sets, optimization seeds or global regions. Overlapping
source sets and shared imagery providers can introduce dependence. Report each
source allocation, raw regional effects and leave-one-region-out sensitivity.
Do not count24 x three allocations x three selections as216 independent regions.

Real-minus-permuted and quota-minus-representative controls are prespecified
secondary descriptive comparisons. Other budgets, metrics, classes, coverage and
pool comparisons are exploratory/descriptive; no family of unadjusted
confirmatory significance claims is planned. No externally justified practical
equivalence margin is available. Report effect ranges and uncertainty without
claiming equivalence from non-significance. Do not tune a margin to the results.

The contribution must be judged after all planned runs, from what the evidence
distinguishes about worst-case coverage, mean representativeness, actual grouping,
source-set variation and representation adaptation. More runs or a completed
draft alone are not evidence of substantive new information. If the extension is
still uninformative, record that scientific limitation and reconsider the question;
do not select a favorable condition or invent a positive mechanism.

## Evidence, storage and release gates

Keep all configurations, selected IDs, code/data identities, finite loss logs and
per-image confusion matrices. Predetermine one full-model checkpoint: source0,
selection0, initialization0, real quota k-center. Preserve it regardless of score.
Its reload predictions must match recorded matrices. Other fits retain full logs
and reconstruction identities; absence of their weights is declared.

The total project limit remains2,000,000,000 logical bytes. Old delivered files,
original images, run evidence and feature indexes are protected. Regenerable
feature arrays and intermediate renderings were inventoried by hash before cache
reclamation. Stream new original TIFFs through RAM, reserve compact-data space,
and stop at failed limits without changing samples or resolution. Later caches
may be reclaimed with the same provenance rules after the recorded computations.

Before a new delivery: verify the entire576-fit matrix; reproduce all numerical
tables from confusion matrices; check target isolation, coverage and georeference
limits; revisit direct literature; independently red-team contribution and
scientific claims; revise all manuscript/supplement/submission artifacts; inspect
every final formatted page and validate the delivered folder. Author identity and
personal declarations can remain explicitly pending. No acceptance is guaranteed.
