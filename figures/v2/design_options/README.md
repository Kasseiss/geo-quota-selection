The prospective randomization amendment pairs selection seed 0/1/2 with optimization initialization 0/1/2 for every formal fit. These are three paired fit seeds, not nine Cartesian seed combinations and not pure selection repetitions at initialization0. Source calibration remains at initialization0, outside the formal fit repetitions. The amendment was made before any new-panel scores in response to a source-only selected-subset diagnostic. Joint seed variation cannot separate selection and optimization variance components.

Different model recipes do not isolate encoder fine-tuning either.
